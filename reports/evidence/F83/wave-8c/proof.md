## 1. In the test runner (jsdom, fake time)

- **The picture of the hand-made motor** was taken BEFORE the engine was touched (commit `test(F83): wave 8c, the picture of the hand-made phone sheet`): 32 scenarios (`test/support/sheetScenarios.js`) played in a host with two `useSheetWindow()`s (`test/support/sheetWorld.js`), 74 KB (`test/fixtures/sheetEngineBeforeF83.json`), taken twice with identical results. Both forms, the open and the close on a fake clock (1 ms, one frame, 199, 200, 240 ms after the close), focus on open and on close (to the opener) and the order of «the page is let go, the focus goes back, whoever opened it is told», Tab and Shift+Tab, Escape (and what the opener under the sheet hears: nothing), a tap outside (bottom only), the drag down with its 80 px (40, 80, 81 px, the header, a move with no start, the body, a full sheet), the screen that stops being a phone, less motion, the page held at its place and given back with the real scroll, a sheet over a sheet, the screen left with a sheet open, `setTitle`, a title with HTML in it, the id counter, a footer that takes the focus as it is drawn, the keyboard of the phone.
- **The check** (`test/ui/SheetWindow.sameAsBefore.test.tsx`, 35 cases): all 32 scenarios against the picture, plus the proof that it is not blind (a word, a class, and the pictures at 199 and 200 ms after a close, which say a sheet that leaves 1 ms early is accused). **Two differences on purpose**, each written in `KNOWN_DIFFERENCES` as «what the old picture becomes» so the rest of the picture is still compared to the letter, and in the header of `ui/SheetWindow.tsx`: (1) a sheet still open when its screen (or the part of it that opened the sheet) is left goes with it at once; (2) a sheet that is closing keeps what was drawn in it while it slides. The same difference (1) shows in the Blend's «a turn of the phone with the sheet open closes it» (declared in `Blend.sameAsBefore`).
- The 13 cases of `test/components/ui/Sheet.test.js` were carried to `test/ui/SheetWindow.test.tsx` through the imperative door `openSheet` (same assertions; a DOM node given as content is a piece of React), plus one for the loop below. The goldens of every screen that opens a sheet (Albums, Inventory, Saved playlists, Playlists, Start, Blend, the shell, the export window) are unchanged and green.
- **Found on the way:** `setTitle` called at every draw (the filters sheet does it in an effect) made the screen draw for ever once the title was state: the suite did not end (a process at 200 % CPU, no message). The title now changes state only when the text is another.

## 2. In the browser, on BUILT sites (parent 1693d71 against this branch)

Builds by `vite build` through the machine queue, `css/`, `assets/`, `js/firebase-config.js` and the favicon copied in as `site-build.sh` does; no `.env` read; Playwright 1.56 (borrowed from MJRPpersonal), a small static server answering deep routes with `app.html`. Firebase hosts and every host except 127.0.0.1 (and the MusicKit stand-in for the Start screen) blocked. Script: `browser-proof.mjs` (`run-all.sh`, `run-extra.sh`, `summarize.py`); raw results `raw/*.json.gz`. Every sheet is opened by a REAL TAP (`tap` with `hasTouch`) and closed by its button, by Escape, by a tap outside and (Chromium) by a drag down on the handle and on the header, through CDP `Input.dispatchTouchEvent` (50 px: snaps back; 140 px and 100 px: closes). Each step compares, parent against branch: the layer (every element, attribute, text node and box), the focus, the page lock and `scrollY` (the page position before and after: no jump), a picture, the errors of the page, and the TIMELINE (the sheet comes, is marked closing, leaves; the page is held and given back), each in ms from the last input before it.

Sheets driven (phone profiles): Create «What is a series?» and its series sheet from the bar (full, with the count in the title); the albums screen (an album's actions, the filters and order); the inventory (an item's actions, the status, the filters and order, the collection's actions, the jump); the saved playlists (a batch's actions, a series' actions, the filters; the batch sheet then «Export»: the export window as a sheet, closed by its button and by Escape); the playlists edit screen (a playlist's sheet, a track's sheet); the phone «Menu» sheet; the question over a sheet (Disconnect Spotify from the menu); and a window that grows past 899 px with a sheet open (6 screens). ## 3. What was NOT proven

- **A real iPhone** (no device here): WebKit through Playwright's iPhone 14 and SE profiles is the engine of Safari, not the phone: the real on-screen keyboard (it only shrinks the window; the proof cannot raise it), the real rubber-band at the edges, the real safe-area insets of a notch phone in landscape (the profiles give the insets of the profile).
- **The drag down with a finger in WebKit:** Playwright's WebKit has no touch drag (the touchscreen only taps). It is proven in Chromium with real touch events through CDP, and by the 32 scenarios of the runner (the touch events of the motor).
- **Not tapped in the browser:** the sheet of the Blend (the step on the phone: the screen needs a series from the services, which the built site does not hand out; the Blend golden has the phone scenarios, 45 in all), the album ranking window as a sheet (`RankWindow`, its phone test `RankWindow.phone.test.tsx` is green), the «Clear album cache» question sheet (the Menu sheet is tapped; its row is not), and the Start screen's sheet with an account signed in.
- Portuguese: nothing in the sheet is text of its own (`closeLabel` and `title` come from the opener, and the door's default is `ui.window.close`); no new string, so no Portuguese to propose.
