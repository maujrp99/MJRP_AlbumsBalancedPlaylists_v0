The Album Blender is a web app for people who listen to whole albums. Gather albums into series, rank every track, and blend them into playlists that keep every record in the mix — then keep count of the ones on your shelf. Open the app Sign in Side A · 2 — Series Gather the albums that belong together. An artist's run, a decade, a mood: a series is a shelf of whole albums you choose. Every blend starts there. Side A · 3 — Ranking Rank every track, not just the singles. Order each album's tracks by how often they are played, by published rankings where the app has them, or by your own ear. The deep cuts get a place in line. Ranking brings the deep cuts back; blending keeps every playlist rooted in albums you chose, whole. Side B · 2 — Start Drop the needle on your first series.
