For whole-album listeners

Dig through the albums you love. Keep every track.

The Album Blender is a web app for people who listen to whole albums. Gather albums into series, rank every track, and blend them into playlists that keep every record in the mix — then keep count of the ones on your shelf.

Side A · 2 — Series

Gather the albums that belong together.

An artist's run, a decade, a mood: a series is a shelf of whole albums you choose. Every blend starts there.

Side A · 3 — Ranking

Rank every track, not just the singles.

Order each album's tracks by how often they are played, by published rankings where the app has them, or by your own ear. The deep cuts get a place in line.

Side A · 4 — Blend

Blend the series into one playlist.

Pick a recipe and the Blender draws tracks from every album in the series, so no record drowns out the rest. You choose how long it runs and what leads.

Side A · 5 — Your shelf

Keep count of the records you own.

Mark the albums on your shelf, see an estimate of what the collection is worth, and what the missing ones would cost.

Side B · 1 — Why whole albums

Shuffle keeps the hits and loses the record. The Blender starts from the record.

An album has an order: an opening, a turn, a closer. Ranking brings the deep cuts back; blending keeps every playlist rooted in albums you chose, whole.

Side B · 2 — Start

Drop the needle on your first series.

Open the app and start a series with one album you love. Sign in to keep your series, rankings and shelf with you.
